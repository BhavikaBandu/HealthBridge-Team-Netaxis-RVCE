# HealthBridge — Demo

**▶ Watch the demo videos:** [Google Drive folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1fHM1TJb_3J6om1189Yb6z32ErhwMCkEG?usp=sharing)

The videos walk through the patient and doctor portals with two synthetic cases:

- **India → UAE:** medicine and MRI matching, lab unit conversion, and UAE controlled-medicine rules.
- **UAE → Switzerland:** medicines matched under different names, a resting ECG matched to the Swiss "Ruhe-EKG", HbA1c conversion, and a flag for exceeding the Swiss 30-day limit on psychotropic medicines.

All patient data shown is synthetic.
